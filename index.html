What is an
Oopbuy spreadsheet?

An Oopbuy spreadsheet is a product research list used to organize shopping links and comparison notes. A useful list records the source, selected variant, measurements, listed price and the date each detail was checked. Oopbuy shopping guide

A useful shortlist starts
with four good decisions.

  1. 01

    Choose a category

    Begin with one need: a sneaker, a warmer layer or an accessory. Keep the search focused.

  2. 02

    Read the listing

    Check the exact color, selected variant, size chart, stated materials and original source link.

  3. 03

    Compare the details

    Put measurements and available photographs side by side. Mark missing information as unknown.

  4. 04

    Check before buying

    Confirm current availability, purchasing eligibility, delivery costs and return terms with the relevant service.

What belongs in your sheet?

A photo can suggest a style. Measurements, source details and current terms help you decide whether a listing fits your needs.

Suggested fields for a personal shopping spreadsheet
CategoryRecord these detailsCheck carefully
Shoes Model description, colorway, size system, size chart and source URL. Whether the chart measures the foot, insole or shoe; do not treat these as interchangeable.
Tops & outerwear Chest width, shoulder, sleeve and garment length; stated fabric and fit. The measurement method and whether the garment is intended for layering.
Pants & shorts Waist range, rise, inseam, leg opening and stated stretch. Flat measurements versus circumference, and any elastic waistband range.
Headwear & accessories Circumference or dimensions, closure type, stated material and care notes. Adjustability, contact materials and the exact variant shown in the listing.
Every shortlisted item Listed price, currency, date checked, source link and unresolved questions. Additional service or delivery charges; an item price alone is not a landed total.

Oopbuy shoes spreadsheet

Group footwear by use and silhouette, then compare the actual size information. Save separate notes for each color and size combination you are considering.

Oopbuy clothing spreadsheet

Build around measurements rather than a familiar size label. Compare a listing with a garment you already own, including how much room you want for movement and layering.

Oopbuy accessories spreadsheet

Record practical details such as dimensions, adjustment and closures. Keep a clear distinction between what a seller states and what you can independently verify.

The short answers

Before you
start browsing.

Clear expectations make a shopping directory more useful. Here is what this page offers and what to verify on the destination listing.

What is an Oopbuy spreadsheet?

An Oopbuy spreadsheet is a product research list used to organize shopping links and comparison notes. A useful list records the source, selected variant, measurements, listed price and the date each detail was checked.

Is this an official Oopbuy website?

No. This is an independent shopping guide with category links to CAFANS. It does not represent Oopbuy, Nike, Louis Vuitton, Gucci or Hermès.

Can I download a spreadsheet here?

This page provides a category directory and shopping checklist, rather than a downloadable spreadsheet. Use the category links to explore CAFANS and record suitable listings in your own shortlist.

Do the photographs show products currently available on CAFANS?

No. The brand photographs are editorial references. They do not confirm CAFANS inventory, authenticity, prices or seller authorization. Check the destination listing for the actual product and its terms.

How should I compare clothing sizes?

Compare the listing's garment measurements with a similar item you already own. Check the measurement method, units, fit and stated tolerance instead of relying only on a letter size.

Does a product link guarantee Oopbuy compatibility?

No. A category or product link alone does not establish platform compatibility. Confirm the original source URL, purchasing eligibility and current restrictions directly with the service you intend to use.
